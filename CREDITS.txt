WHAT IS ON THE PAGE
- The words: from the repository's README and docs.
- The 28 chapter clips: streamed from the repository's own attachments on GitHub (issue #1). They are not copied here.
- Stills of the studio (assets/media/demo, assets/media/chapters): frames of those clips.
- The idea film "A Plan You Can Run" (assets/media/a-plan-you-can-run.*.mp4): made for this site from the same recordings.
  Narration: Microsoft Edge online text-to-speech (en-GB-RyanNeural, zh-CN-YunyangNeural); check Microsoft's terms before
  commercial use. Music: original; samples from the Versilian Community Sample Library (CC0 1.0).
- The 3D model at the top of the page and the plan in the film: derived from the one poster frame the repository publishes
  (assets/media/plate.webp, assets/data/city.json). Stylised screen-space graphics: not the city dataset.
  The traffic drawn on it is an ILLUSTRATION along roads traced from that frame. It is not simulation output.
- The approval replay: the demo's guided fire-services scenario, with the demo's own instructions, prices and travel times.
- The policies.yaml example: abridged from https://challenge.economic-world-model.com/agentic-city.html

MAP DATA SEEN IN THE STUDIO
Compiled, as the repository states, from official Hong Kong mapping and water data (iB1000), OpenStreetMap road and transit
networks ((c) OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL) and public building and land-use records.
